- (decision) Stance: Creative Partner. Techniques batch chosen by coach: Job to Be Done, Role Playing, How Might We, Crazy 8s
- (technique) started Job to Be Done
- (insight by user) Real job-to-be-done: low-effort, fun, low-stakes friendly competition with a handful of touchpoints/season (once pre-season, once pre-playoffs), NOT weekly engagement or dispute-settling
- (idea by user) Reminder emails should ONLY be deadline nudges ('action required until TIMESTAMP') — no result summaries, no newsletter-style content. Manual login for entering predictions/checking standings is fine.
- (idea by user) Hall of Fame page: tracks championship/runner-up/third-place counts across seasons for bragging rights. Each season is self-contained (no other data matters once it ends) but app supports multi-season history via a season-selector dropdown in the UI
- (technique) started Role Playing
- (decision by user) Participant role: own predictions always visible regardless of deadline; other players' predictions visible only after their deadline passes; predictions become fully immutable (frozen) after deadline, no grace period
- (idea by user) Live/projected standings feature: show estimated points from in-progress results (regular season standings, ongoing playoff series scores), sourced via nightly batch sync (no need for real-time) from an official/major provider (NHL.com, ESPN, TSN) — never an unofficial/shaky third-party API
- (decision by user) Scorekeeper role eliminated entirely — fully automated result ingestion from official/major NHL data webservice becomes the sole source of truth for scoring, no manual human confirmation step
- (idea by user) Technical direction (parked for architecture phase): multi-container docker-compose split — storage container, web-UI container, sync container. Sync container fetches results on startup + on cron schedule (interval via env var); resilient to webservice downtime by writing nothing and relying on next scheduled run; no in-app alerting needed since metrics/logs/otel will be exported to Grafana Cloud for monitoring
- (decision by user) Confirmed: only one stakeholder role exists in this app — Participant. Fixed set of 3 (Basti, Sadl, Tobbi), no registration/join flow, no admin/scorekeeper role. Bragging/Hall of Fame is purely among these 3.
- (technique) started How Might We
- (decision by user) Award scoring rule (all 5 awards, incl. Richard/Art Ross): predict 3 names; compare against actual top-3 by ranking, where ties expand the set (e.g. a 3-way tie for 3rd means 5 total 'correct' names); each of the 3 predicted names found anywhere in that set scores 5pts, order-independent, all 3 can hit independently
- (decision by user) Playoff team-mark structure: per conference, 8 teams make playoffs split across its 2 divisions as either 4/4 or 5/3 (real NHL wildcard structure); exactly one division-winner mark per division (4 divisions total)
- (decision by user) Tie-break rule: none — participants with equal Totals share the same rank
- (decision by user) Team identity: teams identified by a stable technical ID (not name), since names/cities change via relocation (e.g. Arizona to Utah); app must support adding new teams easily; a static per-season team list is maintained in the data store (not source code); on creating a new season, available teams are loaded from a webservice and persisted
- (decision by user) Standings show Regular Season points and Playoff points as separate buckets per player; Total (sum of both) determines the winner/ranking. Early Pick and Late Pick (Cup winner, 20pts each) are counted in the Playoffs bucket, not Regular Season, since that's when they actually resolve

- (idea by coach) Screen: Season Hub (landing page after login: current season, your Total, quick links to what's unlocked now)

- (idea by user) UI insight: since there are only 3 players, Standings/Leaderboard doesn't need its own page — show it persistently on every page (e.g. sidebar/header widget)
- (idea by user) Predictions page: single scrollable page showing every player's predictions across all categories (awards, regular season, playoffs); links to 'My Predictions' for entering/updating before deadline
- (decision by user) MoSCoW adjustment: deadline reminder emails move to Must
- (decision by user) MoSCoW adjustment: multi-season data model, season-selector dropdown, and Season Hub landing page all move to Could (not needed for v1)
- (decision by user) MoSCoW adjustment: Hall of Fame page moves to Could
- (decision by user) Terminology correction: the result sync is not literally 'nightly' — it's a cron schedule whose interval is configured via an env var on the sync container
- (decision by user) Dropped: deep per-category point breakdown — Regular Season/Playoffs bucket split is sufficient granularity
- (decision by user) Dropped: separate 'live/projected' standings tier — there is no in-progress-vs-final distinction; the leaderboard is always simply computed from whatever data the last cron sync produced
- (decision by user) Award prediction scope narrowed: only Art Ross and Rocket Richard trophies are included (pure stat leaders — points/goals — available all season via webservice). Hart, Norris, Vezina are left empty/out of scope since official nominees aren't available until late season and the app should not rely on manually curated finalist lists
- (decision by user) Correction: Enter Predictions (Must) includes ALL 5 awards (Hart, Norris, Vezina, Art Ross, Rocket Richard) predicted pre-season, not just Art Ross/Richard. The earlier narrowing was wrong.
- (decision by user) Scoring timing differs per award: Art Ross/Richard can be scored continuously during the season since stat leaderboards are always available; Hart/Norris/Vezina can only be scored once official nominees are published (season end) — this is a data-availability timing constraint, not a scope exclusion
- (decision by user) Core feasibility assumption for v1: reliable, free, public webservices exist for (a) team standings, (b) Art Ross/Richard player stats, (c) playoff pairings/results, (d) other award nominees — preferably NHL official, alternatively ESPN/TSN/other major sports data sources. This assumption is UNVERIFIED and requires investigation.
- (decision by user) As long as the free-public-webservice assumption holds, NO admin/management web page is needed at all — nobody manually enters standings, results, or award nominee data (extends the earlier no-scorekeeper decision to cover awards data too)
- (question by user) Action item: investigate which free, reliable webservices/APIs can supply NHL standings, Art Ross/Richard stats, playoff results, and award nominees — this is load-bearing for the whole no-manual-entry design
- (insight by user) Worked example clarifying tie-inclusive award scoring: top-3 = McDavid(1), MacKinnon(2), then Matthews/Draisaitl/Crosby tied at 46 goals for 3rd. Scenario A: every participant correctly names #1 and #2 but each picks a different one of the three tied-for-3rd players -> everyone scores 3/3 correct despite different picks. Scenario B: a participant misses #1 and #2 entirely but names all three tied-for-3rd players -> also scores 3/3 correct. Confirms: each of a participant's 3 predicted names is checked independently against the full (tie-expanded) correct set, order-independent
- (insight) Synthesis: (1) no-scorekeeper + no-admin-page + resilient-cron-sync are one bet — full trust in external data, risk moved from human typos to webservice reliability, making the unverified free-webservice assumption the top risk item. (2) UI philosophy = transparency without friction (always-visible own picks, post-deadline others, standings everywhere, no-tab scrolling page). (3) Smart sequencing: defer cross-season UI features (Hall of Fame, season-selector) to Could while keeping the cheap one-time technical-ID data model as Must. (4) Predictions vs scoring run on different clocks per award (Art Ross/Richard continuous, Hart/Norris/Vezina only at season end). (5) Overall design principle: remove friction, including social friction between 3 friends (no tie-break, no scorekeeper, no manual entry disputes). Chosen direction: proceed to bmad-deep-recon (technical) to verify free NHL/sports-data webservice availability before finalizing architecture.
