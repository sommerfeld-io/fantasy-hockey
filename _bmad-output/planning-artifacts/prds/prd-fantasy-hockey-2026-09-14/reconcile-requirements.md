# Reconciliation: requirements.md vs. PRD + Addendum

Compares `assets/requirements.md` (258 lines, the source requirements baseline slated for deletion) against `_bmad-output/planning-artifacts/prds/prd-fantasy-hockey-2026-09-14/prd.md` and `.../addendum.md` (the drafted PRD and its addendum), line by line, to confirm every fact, rule, nuance, numeric value, cross-reference, and piece of rationale in the source survives somewhere in the PRD/addendum pair before the source is deleted.

## Gaps found

- **Lines 5-10 (intro) — the general "click-dummy wins on conflict" precedence rule is not restated anywhere.** requirements.md states as a standing rule: "Where the two sources conflicted, the click-dummy wins." PRD §0 (line 13) names the two prior sources (click-dummy prototype, "an earlier, now-retired BMad planning pass") but never states the precedence rule itself — only the five *specific outcomes* it produced are echoed piecemeal through §4/§5 (see next four gaps), with no single place a future reader could learn *why* those specific choices were made, or that a general rule existed at all.

- **Lines 250-258, header (line 252) — the entire "Conflicts resolved in favor of the click-dummy" section's stated *purpose* ("Recorded here for traceability before that planning is retired") is not preserved.** This section exists specifically so the reasoning behind five decisions survives after the earlier `_bmad-output` planning pass (and now requirements.md itself) is gone. Neither prd.md nor addendum.md contains an equivalent traceability log — once requirements.md is deleted, the "earlier planning said X, click-dummy said Y, we picked Y because Z" record disappears entirely, even though the final *outcomes* of 4 of the 5 conflicts do survive (see below).

- **Line 254 — Compare-visibility conflict resolution/rationale not captured.** requirements.md records that earlier planning specified other players' picks stay hidden until a set's deadline, that the click-dummy overrides this (showing "submitted" picks pre-deadline in its own sample data), and that the click-dummy's behavior was deliberately chosen. PRD §4.7 / FR-25–28 and addendum.md state only the final behavior ("no deadline-based hiding") with no mention that this contradicted earlier planning or why the click-dummy's version was preferred.

- **Line 255 — Cup-pick scoring-bucket conflict resolution/rationale not captured.** requirements.md records that earlier planning put *both* Cup predictions (season-opening pick and playoffs re-pick) in the Playoff bucket, and that this document overrides that for the season-opening pick (Regular) per the click-dummy. PRD §4.6 / FR-24's point table states only the final bucket assignment — the fact that this was a resolved conflict with different earlier guidance is lost.

- **Line 256 — Series-length notation conflict resolution/rationale not captured.** requirements.md records that earlier planning specified win-loss notation (e.g. "4-2") for series results, overridden in favor of the click-dummy's game-count-only notation (4-7 games, separate winner field). PRD FR-19/FR-20 and the Glossary's "Series" entry state only the final notation, with no record that an alternative notation was considered and rejected.

- **Line 258 — Finalist-name-validation resolution/rationale not captured.** requirements.md notes the click-dummy left name validation as an explicit open question, and that earlier planning's answer (validated entry, reject non-matching names) was carried forward because it didn't contradict anything the click-dummy asserts as final. PRD FR-17 states only the final rule ("rejects a name that doesn't match") without this provenance — a reader can no longer tell this rule came from the *retired* planning pass rather than the click-dummy itself.

- **Line 58 (US-1.5) — the story-level `[Prototype-validated]` marker is flattened away.** Epic 1 overall is tagged "🆕 New" (the click-dummy has no login flow), but US-1.5 specifically carries its own `[Prototype-validated]` tag (the header showing player name + season, no player switcher, *is* validated by the click-dummy). PRD §4.1's description frames the whole feature as uniformly "Behavior-only" / new, and FR-5 does not flag that its specific consequences (header content, no switcher) are prototype-validated rather than newly specified. Minor related detail: requirements.md line 39 specifies the click-dummy hard-codes the current player "as Basti"; PRD §4.1 drops the name, saying only "hard-codes the current player" (low materiality, noted for completeness).

- **Line 93 (US-2.7) — sample-data deadline detail and its "operational choice, not a fixed rule" framing are dropped.** requirements.md states that in today's click-dummy sample data, all four before-the-season sets share one deadline and the Playoffs Cup re-pick has its own deadline a few days before Round 1's, and explicitly flags this as "an operational choice made per season, not a fixed rule" (i.e., not something to hard-code as a system constraint). PRD FR-12 only states that deadlines are read from `fantasy-hockey.yml` with no settings page — it neither records this sample-data fact nor carries forward the explicit warning against treating the sample pattern as a rule. This matters for a future implementer who might otherwise infer a fixed-deadline-sharing rule from the sample data.

- **Line 242 (Out of scope, bullet 2) — specific enumerated exclusions narrowed to a generic statement.** requirements.md explicitly rules out "no separate admin/scorekeeper account, no spectator/read-only account, and no approval step of any kind." PRD §5 Non-Goals only states "No admin/commissioner role or screen, anywhere" — it does not explicitly rule out a spectator/read-only account or any approval step, both of which are distinct product-boundary decisions in the source that a reader of the PRD alone would not know were deliberately excluded.
